Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how ANO STUDIO CREATIVE ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information and data when you install and use the mobile application AnoRemote: Bluetooth TV Remote (the "Application").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy above all else. AnoRemote does not collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or TV viewing history on our own servers.

2. App Permissions and Hardware Usage

To function as a wireless Bluetooth remote control for Android TV and Google TV, the Application requests specific device permissions. These permissions are used exclusively on your local device:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Used solely to discover, pair, and send Bluetooth HID (Human Interface Device) key commands and text scancodes to your target Smart TV.
  • Vibration Hardware (VIBRATE): Used to trigger haptic feedback vibrations when buttons on the remote control are pressed. You can disable button vibrations anytime in the app settings.
  • Audio Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Referenced for Google Voice Search / Voice Assistant integration on compatible devices. Voice audio is processed by Android system services and is never stored or recorded by AnoRemote.

3. Third-Party Advertising and SDKs

The Application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information and advertising identifiers to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

Data collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • IP Address and generalized coarse location
  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review Google's official privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

5. Security

Because all remote control communications (Bluetooth HID signals) occur directly between your smartphone and your target Smart TV locally via Bluetooth radio waves, your interactions remain strictly private and local to your environment.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications will become effective immediately upon posting on this page.
