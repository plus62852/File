Privacy Policy

App Name: Service Reminder - Maintenance | Package: com.anostudio2.pengingatservis
Summary: Your maintenance records, costs, and photos are stored 100% locally on your phone. We do not transfer or collect your personal items on any external servers.

ANO STUDIO CREATIVE ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Service Reminder - Maintenance mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Local Data Storage & Privacy

We respect your privacy. All data created or uploaded in the App—including item names, service dates, costs, interval schedules, notes, and photos—is stored exclusively in your device's local database (Room Database).

  • We do not collect or store your personal service items on external servers.
  • No user account, email address, or registration is required to use the App.
  • You can export or delete your local records at any time.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The App uses third-party services provided by Google LLC to display advertisements and manage user consent.

Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

We display Banner, Native, and Rewarded Video ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information for advertising purposes, such as:

  • Device Advertising Identifiers (AAID/GAID)
  • IP Address & Approximate Location
  • App Diagnostic & Performance Logs

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage your consent preferences regarding personalized advertising.

To learn more about how Google processes advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

3. Device Permissions Used

The App requests specific Android permissions solely to deliver its core functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): To trigger timely service reminder alerts.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To automatically reschedule local alarm reminders after your phone restarts.
  • VIBRATE: To provide vibration feedback during notification alerts.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To load Google AdMob advertisements and verify internet connectivity.

4. Data Backup & Export

The App offers a CSV export feature allowing you to share or backup your local service records. When using this feature, the exported file is stored in your device's local cache and shared only with the destination application you explicitly select (e.g., Google Drive, WhatsApp, Email, or File Manager).

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Because your data is saved locally on your phone, its security depends on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your mobile operating system updated and securing your device with a PIN or biometric lock.
