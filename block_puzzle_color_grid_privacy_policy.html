1 Introduction & Commitment

Welcome to Block Puzzle: Color Grid (also referred to as "Block Puzzle", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled when you download, install, and play Block Puzzle: Color Grid on Android devices. Our design philosophy prioritizes user privacy through minimal data collection and local-first architecture.

Privacy Summary

We do not require account creation, nor do we collect or store your personal identity information on external servers. Game progress and settings remain local on your device.

2 Information We Collect & Local Storage

Personal Information

We do not collect, harvest, or request any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location. You can enjoy the full gameplay experience completely anonymously.

Local Device Data (DataStore Storage)

To save your game progress and customize your gameplay experience, Block Puzzle: Color Grid saves game data locally on your device using Android's Jetpack DataStore / SharedPreferences framework. This local data includes:

  • Game State & Score: High scores, current game grid layout, active score streaks, and combo statistics.
  • User Preferences: Sound effect toggles, background music volume, and theme selections (Light/Dark mode).
  • Achievement & Progress Data: Unlocked color themes, daily challenge completion status, and gameplay statistics.

This data remains sandboxed within your device's isolated local app storage and is never uploaded to any external servers managed by us. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app will permanently erase this local data.

3 Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep Block Puzzle: Color Grid free to play, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs that may process non-personal diagnostic data and technical device identifiers.

Integrated Third-Party SDKs

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads). AdMob may use device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, coarse location data, and ad interaction metrics to serve ads.
  • Google Play Services: Essential Android operational services, app integrity verification, and device compatibility support.

Google Advertising ID (GAID)

The Google Advertising ID is a resettable, non-permanent device identifier provided by the Android operating system. AdMob uses this identifier to deliver personalized advertisements and prevent fraud.

For detailed information regarding how Google uses data, please review:

Google Privacy Policy | How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4 AdMob & Personalized Ads Opt-Out

You have complete control over whether advertisers use your Google Advertising ID to show personalized interest-based ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time directly through your Android system settings.

How to Opt Out / Reset Advertising ID on Android

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Google Services).
  3. Select Ads.
  4. Tap Reset advertising ID (to generate a fresh random ID) OR tap Delete advertising ID (or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization on older Android versions).

Note: Opting out of ad personalization does not remove ads entirely; rather, ads served will be non-personalized and contextual.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Block Puzzle: Color Grid is built as a general audience puzzle game enjoyed by players of all ages.

COPPA (United States)

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

GDPR & EEA/UK Protection

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK) under 16 years of age will receive contextual, non-personalized advertising unless explicit, verifiable parental consent is granted.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions) has inadvertently provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate measures to delete any such data.

6 Security & Data Storage

We prioritize the security of your device data. Since Block Puzzle: Color Grid relies on local storage rather than cloud-hosted user databases:

  • Android Application Sandboxing: Your saved game progress and preferences are secured by Android's strict application isolation protocols.
  • Encrypted Communications: Any diagnostic or advertising request made by third-party services (such as Google AdMob) is conducted over secure, encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.
  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate external web servers storing personal user profiles, eliminating risks related to central data leaks.
